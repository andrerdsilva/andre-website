André Rodrigues da Silva

Assistant Professor

Federal University of Uberlândia (UFU)

I am a professor at the Federal University of Uberlândia (UFU), Campus Monte Carmelo/MG. Before coming to UFU, I obtained a PhD and a master's in Sanitation and Water Resources, both at State University of Londrina (UEL), and received a BSc in Civil Engineering from UNIFIL in Londrina, Paraná, Brazil. My research focuses on hydrology, fluid mechanics, hydrological and hydrodynamic modeling, and trend analysis of climate extremes.

Interests

  • Streamflow Prediction
  • Climate Change Impacts on Water Resources
